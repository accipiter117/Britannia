# EVENTS / ROME / CHRONICLE

## Event types
Minor: harvest failure, mine collapse, bandits, local dispute.
Major: famine, epidemic, unrest, religious conflict, rebellion.
Historical: Roman consolidation/invasion, faction collapse, later Scandinavian pressure.

Lifecycle:
WARNING → PREPARATION → CRISIS → CONSEQUENCE → NEW NORMAL

Events should preferably arise from current state rather than feel arbitrary.

## Roman chain
Roman Consolidation
→ Roman Presence
→ Military Preparation
→ INVASION WARNING
→ 4-season countdown
→ INVASION
→ Occupation/Resistance
→ New Political Reality

Rome begins outside playable map.
When invasion begins, first army enters via Old Road.
No scripted Roman victory.

Roman AI:
Consolidate → Secure Supply → Advance.

After capturing District:
secure → repair/build → supply → garrison/recruit → advance.

Roman occupation follows same Extract/Autonomy/Integrate rules.

The invasion should disrupt Celtic politics. A faction attacked by Rome may request help, but player can help, ignore, exploit or attack it.

## Rebellion
Occupied → Administered → Integrated.
Low loyalty → Unrest → Rebellion risk → rebel faction.
Maximum two emergent factions simultaneously in prototype.

## Victory
Major Power → Hegemon → Decisive Dominance.
Evaluate territory, population, military, economy, regional influence.
Do not forcibly end just because a hidden percentage was reached.
Offer Continue or End Chronicle when dominance is achieved.

## Defeat
Capital loss does not automatically end campaign.
A faction may retreat/re-establish/reclaim.

## Chronicle
Record:
FOUNDING, BATTLE, VICTORY, DEFEAT, SETTLEMENT_FOUNDED, SETTLEMENT_DESTROYED, FACTION_DEFEATED, ALLIANCE, REBELLION, INVASION, COMMANDER_DEATH, MAJOR_DISASTER, HISTORICAL_DIVERGENCE.

End Chronicle produces a short narrative historical summary.
