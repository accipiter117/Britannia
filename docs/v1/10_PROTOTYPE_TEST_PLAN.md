# TEST PLAN

Primary question:
**Is the seasonal campaign loop fun?**

Test:
1. Do I care about Districts?
2. Does geography matter?
3. Does army position matter?
4. Does supply matter without becoming tedious?
5. Does recruitment have a meaningful economic cost?
6. Are battles meaningful?
7. Does morale matter?
8. Is AI plausible?
9. Does diplomacy matter?
10. Does Roman warning create anticipation?
11. Does Roman invasion change the strategic game?
12. Do I want to press End Season?
13. Can losing players recover?
14. Does Chronicle make the campaign feel like a story?

## First 20 seasonal pressure beats
1 Spring: build Farm
2 Summer: build Road
3 Autumn: see rival/move army
4 Winter: food/army pressure
5 Spring: Warrior Hall
6 Summer: recruit Warriors
7 Autumn: rival movement/diplomatic contact
8 Winter: possible confrontation
9 Spring: first battle opportunity
10 Summer: first tactical battle
11 Autumn: battle consequences
12 Winter: recovery
13 Spring: trade
14 Summer: Roman presence increases
15 Autumn: Roman infrastructure
16 Winter: invasion warning
17 Spring: countdown
18 Summer: Roman mobilisation
19 Autumn: final preparation
20 Winter: invasion

These are tutorial pressure beats, not mandatory player actions.

## Acceptance
Player can complete 20 seasons; build; manage resources; recruit; move; supply; AI; diplomacy; battle; events; Roman invasion; occupation/culture; Chronicle; save/load; desktop/mobile.

Do not perfect balance before the whole loop works.
