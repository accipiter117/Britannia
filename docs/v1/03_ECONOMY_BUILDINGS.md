# ECONOMY / BUILDINGS

## Five resources
Population, Food, Timber, Materials, Wealth.

Population is not currency. Recruitment reduces civilian workforce.

Conceptually:
Population = Workforce + Military + Non-working population

The player does not manually assign workers.

## Approximate base production per season
Fertile: Food 180, Timber 20, Materials 20, Wealth 40
Forest: Food 70, Timber 180, Materials 30, Wealth 25
Hills: Food 60, Timber 60, Materials 150, Wealth 25
Plains: Food 140, Timber 40, Materials 40, Wealth 70
Marsh: Food 80, Timber 50, Materials 30, Wealth 20
Coast: Food 100, Timber 40, Materials 30, Wealth 100

## Consumption
Every 1000 population consumes about 100 Food/season.

Food is the only resource intended to create existential crises.

## Storage
Food/TImber/Materials have storage.
Default food capacity: 1.5 × seasonal consumption.
Granary: 3 × seasonal consumption.
Wealth is a treasury.

## Buildings
Farm: 100 Timber, 50 Materials, 100 Wealth; 1 season; +100 Food.
Granary: 100 Timber, 100 Materials, 100 Wealth; 1 season; increases food storage/winter resilience.
Timber Camp: 100 Timber, 50 Materials, 50 Wealth; 1 season; +120 Timber; requires forest.
Mine: 100 Timber, 150 Materials, 100 Wealth; 2 seasons; +120 Materials; suitable hills/mountains/resource.
Workshop: 100 Timber, 100 Materials, 150 Wealth; 2 seasons; construction efficiency.
Warrior Hall: 150 Timber, 150 Materials, 150 Wealth; 2 seasons; unlocks Warriors/increases military capacity.
Fortification: 200 Timber, 250 Materials, 150 Wealth; 3 seasons; defensive bonus/garrison/siege resistance.
Market: 100 Timber, 100 Materials, 200 Wealth; 2 seasons; +75 Wealth/enables local trade.
Road: 100 Timber, 100 Materials; 1 season; improves movement/supply along connection.
Supply Depot: 100 Timber, 100 Materials, 100 Wealth; 1 season; extends supply.
Harbour and Sacred Site may be included if implementation is simple; no naval/religion simulator.

## Settlement
Village → Town → Major Town.

## Development slots
Wilderness 2; Normal 3; Developed 4; Major strategic 5.

## Seasons
Spring: normal production, good construction/movement.
Summer: strongest general production/campaigning.
Autumn: harvest/stockpiling.
Winter: greater food pressure, slower movement, greater fatigue/attrition if poorly supplied.

Winter should punish poor preparation, not randomly destroy kingdoms.
