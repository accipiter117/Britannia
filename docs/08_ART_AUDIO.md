# ART / AUDIO

## Art
Painted historical diorama / miniature aesthetic.
2.5D, approximately 30° elevated camera.
Painterly, muted earthy palette, natural materials, atmospheric depth, strong silhouettes, mobile readability.
Transparent backgrounds for modular objects.
Consistent upper-left lighting.
No baked text/UI.

Avoid:
satellite realism, flat Civilization tiles, cartoon fantasy, bright national blocks, photorealistic 3D, excessive micro-detail, giant hard borders.

Generate modular assets, not a whole map image.

Approximate later asset groups:
Terrain 10–15
Infrastructure 15–20
Settlement stages 5+
Military 10–15
Landmarks 10–20
UI icons 30–40

## Audio
Music: Peace, Tension, War, Crisis, Victory, Chronicle.
Ambient: forest, rain, wind, marsh, settlement, battlefield.
SFX: construction, movement, battle, recruitment, events, diplomacy, upgrades, season transition.
