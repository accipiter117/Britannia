# BRITANNIA — CLAUDE BUILD HANDOFF

Build a playable browser prototype of Britannia from the supplied specification.

Core goal: prove whether the seasonal campaign loop is fun.

Core loop:
LOOK → IDENTIFY → DECIDE → ACT → SEE CONSEQUENCE → END SEASON → WORLD RESOLUTION → REASSESS

Prototype:
- 10 Districts
- 2 Regions
- 3 Celtic factions
- Roman threat/invasion
- Population, Food, Timber, Materials, Wealth
- Buildings and settlement growth
- Seasonal turns
- Armies, movement and supply
- Compact real-time tactical battles with pause
- Diplomacy
- AI
- Occupation/culture/rebellion
- Events
- Chronicle
- Responsive desktop/mobile UI

Use placeholders first. Final painted art comes later.

DO NOT add major systems not specified. Do not invent a tech tree, dynasties, marriage, detailed religion, naval warfare, multiplayer, huge trade commodity system, Total War-scale battles, or Crusader Kings-style politics.

All numbers are configurable prototype balance values.

Historical dates are NOT hard-coded in Prototype 1. Use Year 1 Spring, Year 1 Summer, etc.

The player can resist or defeat Rome. History creates pressure, not a predetermined outcome.
